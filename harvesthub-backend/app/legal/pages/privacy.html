Privacy Policy

Effective $effective_date

HarvestHub is a marketplace app that connects customers with local farmers. This policy explains what information $company ("HarvestHub", "we") collects when you use the HarvestHub app, how we use and share it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you create an account

  • Your name, email address, phone number, and password. Passwords are stored by our authentication provider in hashed form; we never see them.
  • If you sign in with Google, the name and email address on your Google account.

Customers

  • Your address, which we use to show farms near you. We convert it to map coordinates (see "Who we share it with"). While you type an address, what you've typed is sent to Google Maps to suggest matching addresses.
  • Optional preferences you choose: dietary preferences, the kinds of produce you're interested in, and how you heard about us.
  • Your cart, orders, the pickup or delivery details of each order, and the ratings and comments you leave for farms.

Farmers

  • Farm details: farm name, address and map location, farm types, years in operation, bio, photos, products, prices, and promo codes.
  • Verification information we review before approving a farm: business license number and file, insurance documents, food safety certificates, government-issued ID, proof of land use, certifications, references, and tax ID.
  • Sales, earnings, and payout records.

Payments

Card payments are handled by Stripe. Card details you enter go directly to Stripe; HarvestHub never receives or stores your full card number. We keep a record of each payment (amount, status, and Stripe's reference IDs) and of any refunds.

We keep the dates of your free Harvest Pass month. If you join Harvest Pass, Stripe saves your card so it can charge renewals. We keep your plan, membership status, billing dates, and your card's brand and last four digits (so the app can show which card is on file).

Notifications

If you allow notifications on your phone, we keep a push token for it (an identifier Apple or Google gives the app so we can reach that device), and we remove it when you sign out. We keep a record of the notifications we send you, and your notification choices. Text messages are only sent if you turn them on in the app's notification settings; we record when you did. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to stop them, or turn them off in the app.

Technical information

Our servers keep standard logs (such as IP address, time, and the request made) to operate and secure the service. The app does not use advertising, ad tracking, or third-party analytics, and it does not access your device's location, contacts, camera, or photo library except for files you choose to upload.

How we use information

  • To run the marketplace: show farms and products, process orders and payments, arrange pickup, issue refunds, and pay farmers.
  • To review and approve farmer applications.
  • To send account emails, such as sign-up verification codes, password reset codes, and farmer application decisions.
  • To send notifications about your orders, Harvest Pass, and (for farmers) new orders, application decisions, and payouts — by push notification, email, and, if you turn them on, text message. You can choose which of these you get in the app's notification settings.
  • To provide support, prevent fraud and abuse, keep the service secure, and meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

Who we share it with

  • Farmers you order from. After your order is placed and paid (or placed, for cash on pickup), the farm receives your full name, your phone number, and what you ordered, so they can prepare it and contact you about pickup. You see this notice at checkout before you pay. Farmers stop seeing your phone number once the order is picked up or cancelled.
  • Customers. A farm's name, photos, bio, pickup address, products, and average rating are public in the app. Ratings are shown to farmers without the customer's name.
  • Service providers that process data on our behalf: Supabase (database, sign-in, and file storage), Stripe (payments), Resend (email delivery), Telnyx (text messages, if you turn them on), Expo, Apple, and Google (push notifications), our server hosting provider, Google (if you use Google sign-in), and Google Maps Platform, the U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder, and Zippopotam.us (to suggest addresses as you type and turn an address or ZIP code into map coordinates).
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of HarvestHub, our users, or others.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger or sale, subject to this policy.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your personal information as described on our account deletion page. Records of orders, payments, refunds, and farmer payouts are kept in anonymized form (without your name, phone, or address) for as long as tax and accounting laws require, typically up to seven years. Server logs are kept for a limited time for security.

Your choices and rights

  • You can view and update your profile in the app at any time.
  • You can delete your account in the app, or request deletion on our account deletion page.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain uses. Email $support_email and we'll respond within the time the law requires. We won't treat you differently for exercising these rights.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to verification documents is restricted to the farmer who uploaded them and to HarvestHub staff reviewing applications. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

HarvestHub is for adults. You must be at least 18 to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the effective date above and let you know in the app or by email.
