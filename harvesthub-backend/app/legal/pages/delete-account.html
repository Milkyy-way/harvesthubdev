Delete it in the app

  1. Open the HarvestHub app and log in.
  2. Customers: go to Account → Settings → Delete account.
    Farmers: go to Account → Delete account.
  3. Read what will be deleted, type DELETE, and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away and you're logged out.

Can't use the app?

Email $support_email from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my HarvestHub account". We may ask you to confirm the request, and we'll delete your account within 30 days.

Before you delete: an account can't be deleted while an order is still in progress, or while a farmer has earnings waiting to be paid out or a balance owed from cash orders. Finish or cancel your orders first; farmers can delete their account after their final payout is sent.

What we delete

  • Your login: email address, password, and any Google sign-in connection.
  • Your name and phone number.
  • Customers: your address, preferences, cart, and the comments on your ratings. Harvest Pass ends immediately (no further charges) and your saved cards are removed from our payment provider.
  • Farmers: your farm's address, location, photos, bio, and tax ID; your verification documents and certifications; and your product photos. Your products are removed from the app.
  • If you never placed or received an order, everything else about your account is deleted too.

What we keep, and for how long

Records of orders, payments, refunds, and farmer payouts (amounts, dates, items, star ratings, and the farm name on the order) are kept without your name, phone number, or address, because tax and accounting laws require them, typically for up to seven years. Server logs are kept for a limited time for security. See our Privacy Policy for details.
